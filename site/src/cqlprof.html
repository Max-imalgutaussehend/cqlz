cqlprof: EXPLAIN for CQL --- cqlprof runs SELECT statements with tracing on Cassandra and ScyllaDB and reports tombstones, sstables, replicas, slow steps and latency percentiles. ---

cqlprof

Cassandra has no EXPLAIN, but it has tracing. cqlprof reads the trace and says where the time and the work went.
